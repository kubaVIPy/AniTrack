# 🎌 AniTrack - Live Anime Tracker

A sleek, live broadcast tracker and localized calendar for your current anime watchlist. Automatically sync with your MyAnimeList account or build a custom dashboard using Guest Mode!

**[Visit the Live Site Here!](https://kubavipy.github.io/AniTrack/)**

## Features
* **Localized Calendar:** Converts Japanese anime release times directly into your local timezone.
* **MAL Sync:** Instantly imports your "Currently Watching" list from MyAnimeList.
* **Live Countdowns:** Real-time timers for when your next episodes drop.
* **Guest Mode:** Search the AniList database and build a custom watchlist completely locally.

## Troubleshooting (Blank Screen / Not Loading?)
Because this app fetches live, real-time data directly from the MyAnimeList and AniList databases, **strict adblockers or privacy browsers may mistakenly block the connection.**

If your anime isn't loading or your calendar says *"Nothing airing"*:
1. **Pause your Adblocker** (uBlock Origin, AdBlock Plus, etc.) for this site.
2. If using **Brave**, **Helium**, or **Firefox Strict Mode**, turn off your Privacy Shields/Tracking Protection for this page.
3. Refresh the page! 

*(We don't track your data, but privacy extensions often block third-party API requests by default!)*
